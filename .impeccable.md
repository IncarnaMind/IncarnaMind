## Design Context

### Users

IncarnaMind is a local-first notebook for people writing and checking their own material, including students, analysts, lawyers and teams. This work supports reviewing a recorded meeting without losing the Note or its evidence.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, document-first, checkable. The creator's direction is “quiet paper and ink”: writing dominates, controls recede, and evidence stays easy to inspect.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing system fonts, colours, spacing and motion in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). Keep the established paper-like page and familiar desktop controls; this integration is not a redesign.

### Design Principles

- Keep the Note, its tab, editing state and exact Undo intact when changing views.
- Put progress and problems at the writing they concern.
- Reuse shared menus, viewer loading, keyboard actions and motion.
- Keep controls readable and reachable at narrow and wide widths, in English and Simplified Chinese.
- Treat [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and [the interaction rules](docs/agents/interaction.md) as the source of truth.
