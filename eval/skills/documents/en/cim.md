# Confidential information memorandum — Halden Quay Logistics Limited

January 2024. Prepared for the refinancing of the group's senior facilities. Strictly private and
confidential.

## 1. The group

Halden Quay Logistics Limited is a cold-chain logistics group that stores, handles and distributes
temperature-controlled food for retailers and food manufacturers in the United Kingdom. The group
operates four sites and 40 refrigerated trailers. Its head office is in Hull.

The group is owned 62% by its founder and chief executive, Marta Halden, and 38% by Kestrel Capital
Partners, which invested in 2019. The board is chaired by Anthony Reyes. The finance director is
Priya Nandakumar.

## 2. Investment highlights

- **Revenue grew by 24% in each of the last three years**, and the group has never lost a major
  customer.
- **The group's largest customer accounts for 8% of revenue**, so customer concentration is low.
- **Underlying EBITDA margin has been stable at 22% since 2021**, through energy cycles and the
  pandemic.
- **The group has no material litigation.** It has never had a claim above £100,000.
- **All of the group's properties are owned freehold**, which keeps its cost base fixed and low.
- **The group owns its entire cold-chain fleet**, so it controls capacity and maintenance.
- **The group holds every licence needed for its operations**, and has never had a licence
  suspended.
- **Management expects EBITDA to reach £120 million by 2027** as the Goole depot matures and
  temperature-controlled volumes grow.

## 3. The transaction

The group seeks £240 million of senior facilities to refinance its existing debt, fund the Goole
depot and provide working capital. Merrowgate Bank plc is mandated lead arranger.

## 4. Financial summary

The group's revenue for the year to 31 December 2023 was £468.3 million and underlying EBITDA was
£82.0 million. Net debt at 31 December 2023 was £188.6 million.
