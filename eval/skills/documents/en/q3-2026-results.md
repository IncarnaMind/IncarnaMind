# Halden Quay Logistics Limited — results for the third quarter of 2026

The 13 weeks to 26 September 2026. All figures are unaudited.

## Highlights

Group revenue was £142.6 million, up 8.1% on the third quarter of 2025, when it was £131.9
million. Group gross margin was 27.4%, down from 29.1% a year earlier. Underlying EBITDA was £19.8
million, against £18.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net debt was £196.4 million at the end
of the quarter, and EBITDA for the last twelve months was £70.1 million: net debt to EBITDA was 2.8
times.

Revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was £401.2 million, and underlying EBITDA for the nine
months was £57.2 million.

## What drove the quarter

Cold-store energy costs rose again, and the group absorbed most of the increase rather than pass it
on in full. The new Goole depot opened in July and traded in line with plan. The contract with
Bracken Foods, signed in May, added volume from August.

## Outlook

The board expects full-year revenue of £550 million to £570 million and underlying EBITDA of £74
million to £78 million. This is the guidance given with the half-year results on 30 July 2026,
unchanged. Capital expenditure for the year is expected to be about £38 million, including the
Goole depot.

Marta Halden, Chief Executive: "Volume is where we expected. Margin is not, and energy is most of
the reason. The Goole depot and the Bracken Foods contract give us the volume to grow into next
year."
