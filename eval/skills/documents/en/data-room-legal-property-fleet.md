# Data room extracts — legal, property, fleet and licences

Halden Quay Logistics Limited. Extracts from the data room for the January 2024 refinancing.

## Legal update, 15 December 2023

Pellow Foods Limited has filed a claim in the High Court for £2.4 million, alleging that a
temperature failure at the Goole depot in March 2023 spoiled a consignment. The group is defending
the claim and has notified its insurers. A former depot supervisor has also brought an employment
tribunal claim for unfair dismissal; the group is defending it. No other proceedings are known.

## Property schedule, December 2023

- Head office, Hull: owned freehold.
- Goole cold store: leased from Ravensworth Estates Limited, lease dated 1 July 2022, 20-year term.
- Motherwell cold store: leased from Clyde Pension Trustees, lease dated 14 March 2018, 15-year
  term.
- Avonmouth cold store: leased from Severn Logistics Park Limited, lease dated 9 September 2020,
  12-year term.

## Fleet schedule, December 2023

The group operates 40 refrigerated trailers. Of these, 28 are owned outright and 12 are held under
finance leases with Merrowgate Equipment Finance Limited, each with a four-year term ending in 2026
or 2027. The group owns six tractor units and leases the remainder from a haulage contractor.

## Licence and registration register, December 2023

- Food business registrations for the Hull, Goole, Motherwell and Avonmouth sites: current.
- Cold-store operator approvals for the Goole and Motherwell sites: current.
- Waste carrier registration: current.
- Goods vehicle operator licences: current for the group's operating centres.
- Environmental permits for refrigeration plant at Hull and Goole: current.

No licence or registration has been suspended, revoked or made subject to enforcement action in
the last five years.
