# Data room extracts — financials and customers

Halden Quay Logistics Limited. Extracts from the data room for the January 2024 refinancing.

## Management accounts, years ended 31 December

- 2021: revenue £382.4 million; underlying EBITDA £76.5 million.
- 2022: revenue £410.1 million; underlying EBITDA £78.8 million.
- 2023: revenue £468.3 million; underlying EBITDA £82.0 million.

The accounts state that revenue grew by 7.2% in 2022 and by 14.2% in 2023, and that the underlying
EBITDA margin was 20.0% in 2021, 19.2% in 2022 and 17.5% in 2023.

## Customer schedule, year ended 31 December 2023

- Bracken Foods Limited: £88.9 million, 19.0% of revenue.
- Northfield Grocers plc: £61.4 million, 13.1% of revenue.
- Calder Vale Convenience Limited: £42.2 million, 9.0% of revenue.
- All other customers: £275.8 million, 58.9% of revenue.

The schedule states that the ten largest customers accounted for 61.5% of revenue in 2023.

## Aged debtors

Trade debtors over 60 days were £6.2 million at 31 December 2023, against £3.1 million a year
earlier. The accounts attribute the increase to two retail customers and a change in payment terms.
