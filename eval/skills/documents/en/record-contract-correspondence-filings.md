# Record — contract, correspondence and court filings

Ravensworth Analytics Ltd v Kestrel Health Systems Ltd, Claim No. KB-2024-000418, High Court of
Justice, King's Bench Division.

## Services agreement

The services agreement between Ravensworth Analytics Ltd and Kestrel Health Systems Ltd is dated 3
March 2023. It took effect on 1 April 2023 and had an initial term of 12 months. Kestrel was to
provide a data analytics service for Ravensworth's warehouse network for £40,000 a month, and the
first delivery was due on 30 June 2023.

## Purchase order

Ravensworth issued purchase order RA-2023-017 on 14 April 2023.

## Email of 12 June 2023

Dana Whitlock, Kestrel's Head of Operations, wrote to Ravensworth on 12 June 2023: "We have not yet
started the build."

## Delivery note

Kestrel's delivery note dated 28 July 2023 records that the first dashboard was delivered on 28
July 2023.

## Letter of 2 August 2023

Kestrel's letter of 2 August 2023 says that delivery "took place on 29 July 2023", and that the
delay was caused by late data from Ravensworth.

## Complaint of 19 September 2023

Ravensworth complained by email on 19 September 2023 that the dashboard's stock figures were not
refreshing.

## Fix of 2 October 2023

Dana Whitlock's email of 2 October 2023 says that the fix was live.

## Termination notice

Ravensworth's termination notice is dated 6 November 2023. The covering email that sent it is dated
7 November 2023.

## Claim form

The claim form was issued on 22 January 2024.

## Defence

Kestrel served its defence on 18 March 2024.

## Case management order

The court made a case management order on 5 June 2024. It listed disclosure for 28 February 2025,
witness statements for 30 May 2025 and trial for 14 July 2026.

## Undated file note

An undated file note records a call between Dana Whitlock and Ravensworth's operations manager about
the September dashboard. The note gives no date for the call.
