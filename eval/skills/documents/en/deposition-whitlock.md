# Deposition of Dana Whitlock — 12 March 2026

Ravensworth Analytics Ltd v Kestrel Health Systems Ltd, Claim No. KB-2024-000418, High Court of
Justice, King's Bench Division. Taken at the offices of Ashby & Rowe LLP, Leeds, before Rebecca
Oduya, court reporter. Appearances: Martin Ellis for Ravensworth Analytics Ltd; Sofia Grant for
Kestrel Health Systems Ltd.

Excerpt: pages 12 to 17.

## Page 12

1  Q. Please state your full name.
2  A. Dana Whitlock.
3  Q. What was your role at Kestrel Health Systems Ltd?
4  A. I was Head of Operations from March 2023 until November 2023.
5  Q. What did that role cover?
6  A. Delivery, customer support and the operations team. I reported to the chief executive.
7  Q. Did you have responsibility for the Ravensworth account?
8  A. Yes. I was the day-to-day contact from the signing until I left.

## Page 13

1  Q. When was the services agreement signed?
2  A. It was signed on 3 March 2023. I was not in the room, but I saw the signed copy.
3  Q. What did Kestrel agree to provide?
4  A. A data analytics service for Ravensworth's warehouse network, for £40,000 a month.
5  Q. When was the first delivery due?
6  A. The statement of work said 30 June 2023.
7  Q. Was it delivered on that date?
8  A. No. We delivered on 28 July 2023.
9  Q. Why was it late?
10 A. Ravensworth was late giving us the warehouse data. We could not build without it.

## Page 14

1  Q. You say Ravensworth was late with the data. When did you ask for it?
2  A. In May 2023, I think.
3  Q. Do you see the email of 12 June 2023 at bundle page 214?
4  A. Yes.
5  Q. It is an email from you, is it not?
6  A. Yes.
7  Q. It says, "We have not yet started the build." Do you see that?
8  A. I do.
9  Q. So on 12 June 2023 Kestrel had not started, although delivery was due on 30 June?
10 A. That is what the email says. I do not remember the delay being all Ravensworth's.
11 MR. ELLIS: I have no further questions on that document.

## Page 15

1  Q. Let us turn to the September dashboard. Do you see the email of 19 September 2023?
2  A. Yes. Ravensworth said the dashboard was wrong.
3  Q. Was it fit for purpose?
4  A. The September dashboard was not fit for purpose. I accept that.
5  Q. What was wrong with it?
6  A. The stock figures were not refreshing. The team fixed it by 2 October 2023.
7  Q. Did you tell Ravensworth that?
8  A. I told them on 2 October that the fix was live.

## Page 16

1  Q. When did Ravensworth terminate the agreement?
2  A. By notice dated 6 November 2023. I saw it on 7 November.
3  Q. Did Kestrel accept the termination?
4  A. No. We treated it as wrongful. The agreement had no right to terminate for that.
5  Q. Did Kestrel stop work?
6  A. Yes, on 7 November 2023. We stopped the following morning.
7  Q. Did Kestrel offer to continue?
8  A. I do not recall an offer to continue.

## Page 17

1  Q. Ravensworth claims £310,000. What is that figure?
2  A. I do not know how they calculate it.
3  Q. What did Kestrel spend on the account?
4  A. About £180,000 in staff and platform costs.
5  Q. Did Kestrel offer a refund of any fees?
6  A. I do not recall a refund.
7  Q. Did anyone at Kestrel discuss a refund with you?
8  A. Not that I remember.
