# Senior facilities agreement — Halden Quay Logistics Limited

Excerpt: clauses 1, 2, 7 and 8. Dated 14 February 2024.

This agreement is made between Halden Quay Logistics Limited as the Company, the banks named in
Schedule 1 as the Lenders, and Merrowgate Bank plc as the Agent.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

- "Consolidated Net Debt" means, at any date, the aggregate borrowings of the Group less its cash
  and cash equivalents, as shown in the Compliance Certificate for the Relevant Period.
- "EBITDA" means, in respect of any Relevant Period, the consolidated operating profit of the Group
  before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation, as shown in the Compliance Certificate for
  that Relevant Period.
- "Leverage" means, in respect of any Relevant Period, the ratio of Consolidated Net Debt on the
  last day of that Relevant Period to EBITDA in respect of that Relevant Period.
- "Net Finance Charges" means, in respect of any Relevant Period, the interest and other finance
  charges of the Group less interest received, as shown in the Compliance Certificate.
- "Quarter Date" means the last day of each of the Company's financial quarters.
- "Relevant Period" means each period of twelve months ending on a Quarter Date.
- "Termination Date" means 14 February 2029.

## 2. The facilities

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Lenders make available to the Company:

(a) a sterling term loan facility in an aggregate amount of £180,000,000 ("Facility A"); and

(b) a sterling revolving credit facility in an aggregate amount of £60,000,000 ("Facility B").

2.2 The Company shall apply all amounts borrowed under the Facilities towards its general corporate
purposes and those of the Group, including the refinancing of its existing debt and capital
expenditure on cold-store capacity.

## 7. Financial covenants

7.1 Leverage. The Company shall ensure that Leverage in respect of each Relevant Period does not
exceed 3.25:1.

7.2 Interest cover. The Company shall ensure that the ratio of EBITDA to Net Finance Charges in
respect of each Relevant Period is not less than 3.5:1.

7.3 Testing. The financial covenants shall be tested on each Quarter Date by reference to the
Compliance Certificate delivered with the Company's quarterly financial statements.

## 8. Repayment

8.1 The Company shall repay each Loan in full on the Termination Date.

8.2 The Company may not reborrow any part of Facility A which is repaid.
