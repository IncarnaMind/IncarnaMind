# Halden Quay Logistics Limited — results for the second quarter of 2026

The 13 weeks to 27 June 2026. All figures are unaudited.

## Highlights

Group revenue was £138.4 million, up 7.4% on the second quarter of 2025. Group gross margin was
28.0%, down from 29.4% a year earlier. Underlying EBITDA was £19.1 million, against £17.9 million
in the second quarter of 2025. Net debt was £193.8 million at the end of the quarter.

Revenue for the first six months of 2026 was £258.6 million, and underlying EBITDA for the six
months was £37.4 million.

## Outlook

The board expects full-year revenue of £550 million to £570 million and underlying EBITDA of £74
million to £78 million. Trading in the first half was in line with the board's expectations, with
energy costs the main pressure on margin.
