# Merrow Strait Ferries: annual figures 2025

Merrow Strait Ferries Ltd runs two routes from Harlow Quay: the West route to Penvarra and the East route to Skerran Isle. The figures are for the calendar year 2025 and are unaudited.

## Traffic

| Route | Crossings | Passengers | Vehicles |
|---|---|---|---|
| West (Harlow Quay to Penvarra) | 6,120 | 412,600 | 61,200 |
| East (Harlow Quay to Skerran Isle) | 3,720 | 187,300 | 22,450 |
| Total | 9,840 | 599,900 | 83,650 |

West route passengers rose from 398,100 in 2024. East route passengers fell from 191,900 in 2024, after eleven winter days were cancelled for storms.

Punctuality was 96.2% on the West route and 93.8% on the East route.

## Income

Fare income was £6.84 million on the West route and £2.91 million on the East route, £9.75 million in all. Freight brought in a further £1.42 million, so total income was £11.17 million.

## Costs

Operating costs were £6.88 million: crew £3.10 million, fuel £2.36 million, maintenance £0.88 million and port dues £0.54 million.

The fleet burned 3,450 tonnes of marine gas oil in 2025, at an average of £684 per tonne.

Operating profit was £4.29 million, a margin of 38.4% of total income.
