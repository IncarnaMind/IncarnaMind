# Fuel supply agreement: Merrow Strait Ferries Ltd and Corran Marine Fuels Ltd

Signed at Harlow Quay on 3 March 2025.

1. Term. This agreement runs from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2028.

2. Fuel. Corran Marine Fuels Ltd supplies marine gas oil, delivered by road tanker to the fuel berth at Harlow Quay.

3. Price. The price per tonne is the monthly average of the published Northwest Europe gas oil index plus a fixed margin of £38 per tonne. From 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 the price will not exceed £700 per tonne.

4. Volume. Merrow Strait Ferries Ltd will take at least 3,000 tonnes in each contract year. If it takes more than 3,500 tonnes in a contract year, Corran Marine Fuels Ltd gives a discount of 2.5% on the price of the fuel above 3,500 tonnes.

5. Payment. Invoices are paid within 30 days of delivery.

6. Ending the agreement. Either party may end this agreement by six months' written notice given after 31 March 2027.
