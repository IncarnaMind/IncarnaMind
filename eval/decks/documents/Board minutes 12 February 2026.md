# Merrow Strait Ferries Ltd: minutes of the board meeting of 12 February 2026

Present: six directors, with the finance director and the company secretary in attendance. The meeting was held at Harlow Quay.

1. Results for 2025. The finance director presented the unaudited figures for 2025. Total income was £11.17 million and operating profit £4.29 million. Fuel cost £2.36 million against a budget of £2.20 million.

2. Fares. The board approved a fare increase of 4.5% on both routes from 1 April 2026.

3. Capital budget. The board approved a capital budget of £3.2 million for 2026: £2.1 million for the refit of the MV Kestrel and £1.1 million for new ticket gates at Harlow Quay.

4. Fuel. The board asked the finance director to obtain a second quotation for fuel supply by 30 June 2026, before the notice period under the Corran agreement opens.

5. Dividend. The board declared a dividend of £0.9 million, payable on 31 March 2026.

6. Next meeting. The next meeting will be held on 14 May 2026.
