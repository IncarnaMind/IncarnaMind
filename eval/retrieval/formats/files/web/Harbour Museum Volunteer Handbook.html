Harbour Museum Volunteer Handbook

This handbook is for volunteers on the weekday rota and a paper copy is kept at the front desk.

Opening the galleries

The east gallery opens at nine thirty on weekdays, half an hour before the main doors.

Switch on the case lights in the coin room last, after the cleaners have left.

Room checklist

The closing volunteer walks the building once and signs the checklist at the desk.

What to bring

Wear the green lanyard and keep the radio on channel two.

  • Bring the blue clipboard with the visitor tally sheet.
  • Carry the spare keys for the east gallery.
  • Keep a torch in the front desk drawer.
