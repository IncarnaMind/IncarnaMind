Riverbank Allotment Water Plan

The plan covers the forty-eight plots on the east and west banks and is reviewed each spring by the allotment committee.

Watering schedule

Plots on the east bank are watered on Tuesday and Friday evenings, between six and eight.

The west bank keeps its old schedule, with watering on Sunday mornings only, unless the tank is below half.

Tank and pump records

Every tank is inspected once a year, and the pump is serviced in the same visit.

Before the frost

Complete this list before the first hard frost, which usually comes in late November.

  • Drain the hoses and store them in the tool shed.
  • Lag the outside tap beside the pump house.
  • Bring the spare watering cans indoors.
