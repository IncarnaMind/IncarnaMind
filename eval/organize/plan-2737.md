## The rule

A run's measures are the eval's own:
- Filed: readable Documents in a Folder, over readable Documents;
- different-label pairs sharing a Folder, over different-label pairs;
- same-label pairs together with the split allowed;
- the smallest Folder.

A mean is the mean of the five runs' shares. Every run counts, and there is no tolerance.

1. **An arm qualifies** when, in English and in Chinese alike, both of these hold:
   - its mean different-label share is no higher than `main`'s mean;
   - its mean Filed is no lower than `main`'s mean.
2. **Of the arms that qualify, the one chosen** has the highest Filed, as the mean of its English and Chinese means. A tie goes to the lower different-label share, measured the same way. A tie there goes to the arm with fewer parts.
3. **If no arm qualifies, none is chosen.** #2917 then drops both parts, and the result is reported as it is.
4. **The report gives every run's numbers,** and beside each mean `main`'s lowest and highest run.
