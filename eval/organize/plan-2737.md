## Runs

- **Isolation stage.** The tuning half (`INCARNAMIND_ORGANIZE_SPLIT=tune`), one language at a time:
  - English Documents with the interface in English (23 Documents, 21 readable);
  - Chinese Documents with the interface in Chinese (17 Documents, 16 readable).
- **Not used for choosing:** neither the fresh set nor the old held-out 40.
- **Five runs an arm and language,** on Claude Haiku 5.5 with the key `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`.
- **One run at a time, in turns:** run 1 of `main`, part 2, part 2b, part 3 and both in English, then the same in Chinese, then run 2, and so on to run 5. If "both, 2b" runs, it runs afterwards, in turns with five more runs of `main` (see Q3).
- **Every run is reported.** A run that ends for a technical reason (an error, not a bar) is run once more, and both are reported. Runs are added under point 5 only, never replaced, and never repeated for their numbers.

## The rule

1. **Five runs an arm, each language, at every stage:** the isolation stage and the final gate.
2. **The tolerance** is `main`'s standard error at that stage: the sample standard deviation of `main`'s five runs (n − 1), over the square root of five, with a floor of one percentage point. It is computed for each measure and language from `main`'s runs at that stage (see Q5).
3. **Different-label pairs:** the arm's mean is no higher than `main`'s mean plus the tolerance. **Filed:** the arm's mean is no lower than `main`'s mean minus the tolerance. Both are read with the second Folder honoured; the labels as written are reported beside them.
4. **The 10% bar on the 40 in Chinese** holds on every single run, with no tolerance. The 40 runs only at the final gate, so this point applies there (see Q4).
5. **One extension, at most once a comparison.** If an arm lands between one and two standard errors on the wrong side of point 3, five more runs are made for that arm and for `main`. It is judged on all ten by the same rule, the standard error recomputed from `main`'s ten. Beyond two standard errors it fails. Whether this extension also applies to point 6 is Q1.
6. **A second-look arm** (part 2, part 2b, and both arms) qualifies only if both of these hold, and level with `main` it is dropped, not merged:
   - its Filed mean is above `main`'s mean plus the tolerance in at least one language;
   - in the other language it is not below `main`'s mean minus the tolerance.
7. **Part 3** qualifies when it is level under points 3 and 4: the examples taken from the tuning set shouldn't stay, so level is enough for it.
8. **The both arms** are judged as second-look arms, so point 6 applies to them. "Both, 2b" is judged only if part 2b qualifies alone.

## Choosing

- **More than one qualifies:** the arm with the highest Filed is chosen, as the mean of its English and Chinese means. A tie goes to the lower different-label mean, measured the same way. A tie there goes to the arm with fewer parts.
- **No second-look arm qualifies:** part 3 alone goes forward if it qualifies.
- **Nothing qualifies:** nothing goes forward. #2917 drops both parts, and the result is reported as it is.
- **The report gives every run's numbers,** and beside each mean `main`'s lowest and highest run and the tolerance.

## The final gate, once

The arm chosen runs beside `main`, five runs each, in turns:
- the fresh set: English Documents with the interface in English, Chinese with it in Chinese;
- the old held-out 40: all 40 Documents with the interface in English, then in Chinese.

Only the aggregate lines are read. It merges only when, on each of those sets and languages, points 3 and 4 hold, with point 5's extension available once (whether point 6 applies here too is Q2). If it fails, it doesn't merge, and that is reported. This round changes nothing in the choice.
